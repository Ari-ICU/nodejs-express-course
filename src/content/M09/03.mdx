### 1. ច្បាប់ដាក់ឈ្មោះធនធាន
* ** ប្រើនាមពហុវចនៈ **៖ តែងតែដាក់ឈ្មោះCollectionsធនធានជាមួយនាមពហុវចនៈអក្សរតូច (ឧ. \\`/users\`, \`/articles\`, \`/orders\`). Never use verbs in paths like \`/getUsers\` or \`/createOrder\`.
* **Kebab-case in URIs**: Use lowercase with hyphens for multi-word paths (\`/payment-methods\`, \`/shipping-addresses\`), never camelCase or snake_case.
* **Hierarchical Nesting for Relations**: Express parent-child relationships through natural URL paths: \`/users/15/orders/302\`. Limit nesting to a maximum of **2 levels deep** to prevent unwieldy URLs; flatten subsequent lookups to root endpoints.
