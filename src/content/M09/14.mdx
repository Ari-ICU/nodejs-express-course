### 1. Offset-Based Pagination Formula
Clients pass two URL query parameters:
* **\`page\`**: The target page number (1-indexed).
* **\`limit\`**: The number of items to return per page.
* **Skip Calculation**: \`skip = (page - 1) * limit\`.
* In MongoDB: \`collection.find().skip(skip).limit(limit)\`.
* In SQL: \`SELECT * FROM items LIMIT ? OFFSET ?\`.
